An AI front desk for a dental office

Demo project. Brightside Family Dental is a fictional business built to show what an AI chat assistant and lead automation look like on a real small-business site.

The problem

Small offices miss calls after hours and during procedures. Patients ask the same few questions (hours, prices, insurance, how to book), and a missed question often becomes a lost patient.

What I built

  • A chat assistant on the website that answers from the office's own information and refuses to guess.
  • Lead capture inside the conversation: name, contact details and the reason for the visit.
  • An automation that saves each lead to the CRM and alerts the front desk in Slack.

Design choices

  • Answers stay inside the facts. The assistant only states hours, prices and policies it was given, and sends everything else to a person.
  • No medical advice. Symptoms and emergencies get a short safety message and the office contact path.
  • No duplicate contacts. The CRM step updates existing contacts by email, so repeat visitors do not create duplicates.

Stack

n8n, Claude (Anthropic API), HubSpot, Slack, a static site on Azure Static Web Apps.
